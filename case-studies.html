Case studies · Cvent CONNECT Europe

what changed, and why it worked.

Two stories from Cvent's flagship European user conference, which draws more than 3,000 registrants a year.

01

from generic to segmented

+148%

email-driven registrations, 2025

The problem

The messaging was generic, so people scrolled past it.

What I did

I segmented the audience by role, sector, and client versus prospect, and rewrote every channel around what each group cared about. To tailor at that scale, I built an AI agent that produced personalised, on-brand content for each segment. Then I tested continuously.

The result

Segmented emails outperformed combined sends, and email-driven registrations more than doubled in 2025.

02

free passes, empty seats

73%

show rate, up from 64%

+27%

registration revenue, 2026

The problem

Show rates were sliding as complimentary passes grew. In 2025, comp passes had a 37% no-show rate, against 6–9% for paid passes.

What I did

I spotted the pattern in the data and helped drive a change to the complimentary pass policy for 2026.

The result

Fewer registrations by design, but the room was just as full. Show rate rose to 73% and registration revenue grew 27%.
