About

hi, i'm oonagh.

The "Oon" in Oonique. Say it oo-NEEK.

I've been a freelance event marketer since 2022, working with names like Cvent, Reuters and Informa. My job has usually been the same: inherit marketing that's functional but flat, work out why it isn't landing, and fix it.

My longest client has been Cvent. From 2023 to 2026 I led registration marketing for Cvent CONNECT Europe, their flagship European user conference. I rebuilt generic messaging into segmented campaigns that people actually responded to, and used the data to spot that free passes were filling registration lists but not rooms.

I started Oonique because that kind of thinking is what makes the difference, and too many event teams are stuck firefighting rather than stepping back. I bring an outside view, clear recommendations and, if you want it, the hands to deliver them.
