How I work

What sits behind the services.

Data analysis

I go past registration totals: show rate by ticket type, session attendance and feedback by segment, registration pace against last year. That's where the real problems show up.

Testing

Every send is a test. I change one thing at a time, by segment, and keep a record of what each audience responds to, so next year starts from evidence.

AI agents and workflows

I build AI agents and workflows that draft personalised, on-brand content for each audience, repurpose it across channels and speed up analysis. The insight still comes from people. AI makes it scale.

Copy and tone of voice

I write the copy as well as the strategy, and I'll build a tone of voice your whole team can write in.

Video

Short, phone-shot, personality-led video that looks like it came from a person, not a brand.

Sales and partner enablement

Sales and customer success talk to your audience every day. I give them tracking links, talk tracks, promo slides and clear discount rules so they can sell the event too.
